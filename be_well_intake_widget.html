Patient Details
Clinical History
💬 Conversation Guide▼
  • Ask what's bringing them to Be Well Kentucky
  • Invite them to share their goals for treatment
  • Ask about current medications, therapist, or diagnosis if not already known
  • Ask about suicidal thoughts/intentions — be direct but gentle
💬 Conversation Guide▼
  • Explain that we coordinate care with their other providers
  • Ask for verbal ROI consent to contact their providers
  • Get primary care doctor — name, practice, phone, email
  • Ask about any other providers: psychiatrist, dietitian, specialist
ED Questionnaire
💬 Conversation Guide▼
  • Let them know you need a few clinical questions to find the right level of care
  • Ask about eating habits, weight changes, and relationship with food
  • Keep questions conversational — this is screening, not diagnosis
  • After gathering info: "Our next steps are a clinical consult, medical labs, and insurance verification"
📋 Next Steps — Read to Patient
"Thank you for sharing that. Here are the next steps: We will schedule an initial clinical consult, and we will also need you to complete routine medical labs. While you do that, we will verify your insurance benefits, and then we can finalize your intake!"

Be Well Kentucky — Payment Options
💳 Standard Self-Pay Rate $[rate] / session ▼
Be Well Kentucky is a private-pay practice. Our standard session fee is $[rate] for a 50-minute individual session. Payment is due at time of service via credit card, HSA, or FSA.
🌐 Out-of-Network Insurance Reimbursement Varies by plan ▼
While we do not bill insurance directly, many clients receive partial reimbursement through their out-of-network benefits. We provide a superbill you can submit to your insurance company. Ask your insurer: "What are my out-of-network outpatient mental health benefits?"
🎓 Practicum Student Rate $[rate] / session ▼
Clients who work with our supervised practicum students receive a reduced rate of $[rate] per session. Practicum students are graduate-level clinicians completing their training under direct supervision. Availability is limited.
📋 Reduced Fee Application Based on income ▼
Be Well Kentucky offers a limited number of reduced fee slots for clients who demonstrate financial need, determined on a sliding scale based on household income and family size. The client must complete our Reduced Fee Application. Slots are limited and not guaranteed.
